How It Began

The Neighborhood Needed This

Cozy Mug opened on Corona Avenue with one goal: give the neighborhood a beautiful place to sip, snack and slow down.

We filled a bright, modern room with marble tables, navy chairs and comfy couches, stocked the pastry case with croissants, danishes, cookies and cakes, and built a menu with something for everyone — from a $3.99 coffee and a $4.50 egg sandwich to milkshakes, smoothies and mojito refreshers.

One guest called us “a little gem by the park” — we'll take it.

Our Philosophy

We believe in premium coffee, pastries that are fresh and never too sweet, and prices that let you come back every day.
