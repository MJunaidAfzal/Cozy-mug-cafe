Our Story

A Cozy Corner for the Neighborhood

Cozy Mug is a bright, modern café where good coffee, fresh pastries and friendly faces come together — every day from morning to night.

Marble tables, navy velvet chairs and big sunny windows. Plenty of seats, comfy couches and charging outlets, so you can meet a friend, get some work done or simply slow down with a latte and something sweet from the case.
